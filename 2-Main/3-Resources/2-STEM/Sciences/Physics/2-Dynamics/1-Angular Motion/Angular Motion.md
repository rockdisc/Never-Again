#Science/Physics/Classical 

Spin means to move along an internal axis, orbit is external.

Angular Displacement:
![[Angular Displacement]]

as the same with [[Velocity]], it is measured in displacement over time, for this though it is most often called [[Average Angular Velocity]]
![[Average Angular Velocity#^ec9953]]
Measured in radians per second or rpm.

The angular acceleration is the same as regular [[Acceleration]]:

$$
\alpha = \frac{\delta \omega}{\delta t}
$$
Rotational kinematics takes direct equations from linear [[Kinematics]], though with rotational values, this means that the equations will translate over. 
$$
\begin{align}
\omega _{f}= \omega_{i} +  \alpha t  \\
\Delta \theta=\omega_{i}t+\frac{1}{2} \alpha t^2 \\
\omega^2_{f}=\omega ^2_{i}+2\alpha \Delta \theta
\end{align}

$$

This also goes into dynamics with things like $F=ma$ turning into:
$$
\sum\tau=I \alpha
$$
