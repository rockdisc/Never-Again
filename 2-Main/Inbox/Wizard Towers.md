#Worldbuilding/CSULT

Wherever these [[Wizards(CSULT)]] are in the world and whatever they call themselves, they organize into groups. Humans are social creatures, and so are the Wizards. These are called towers. This is mainly for the Eastern side of the North Continent, though some have spread in the south. There are the Orthodox towers and the heresies, non-orthodox. There can only be 22 Orthodox towers at one time to mitigate [[Pleroma]] damage and understanding, though that is only the surface reason. It also allows for the higher powers to more easily control the towers by associating them with [[Symbolism]]s. This also helps the wizards a bit as well as they are able to use those symbolisms for their own benefits. These are organized in the [[Kabbalah]]ic Tree of Life, though they do not know this for themselves, but they [[Gods(CSULT)]] refer to them as such. It is a cosmic [[Symbolism]].
