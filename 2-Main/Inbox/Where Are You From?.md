#Speech 

That question in itself brings a few problems: first is thinking I am not from here, color of my skin; next is what the people around me want me to answer; last is that it is a very complex question, especially to me. As an immigrant, I have lived in this country for as long as I can remember, legitimately, my earliest memory is in America. But I was not born here, but instead in India, and my culture and heritage is from India, but I have assimilated into the culture of Kansas. When I go on road trips I have to think to myself what the person is asking, if they want where I live or where my family is from.
