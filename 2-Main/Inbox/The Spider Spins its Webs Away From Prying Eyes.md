#Writing/CSULT 

"Why were you awake?" I asked getting more comfortable in my chair. The woman in front of my was of the fickle variety; this is her second day here yet she it but to give a straightforward answer. 
"How many spiders do you think are in this inn? It does not matter the specific, but I am sure it is not what you see on the average. Only when cleaning or going out of our way to do something unnatural is when we find the truth. They are cunning creatures, they know your schedule, adapt to it, they hide when in sight and hunt when unseen. Their webs are built far from prying eyes. Yet humans are more cunning." She peered into my eyes as though looking through all of me and past that as well. I did not think someone could stare that hard, but she was doing it as though natural, her pinpoint pupils forced me to stare back. I almost forgot to listen to her next words. "As I said before, I am but a travelling scribe, I remember for those who cannot, but I remember all, not just what one wants me to see." She turned her head toward the window, I exhaled for the first time in forever. "Humans are like spiders, once their web is set up in an unseen corner, they think it is safe. Once they know the human's schedule, they think it will never change. The best time to know what people are hiding, is to watch them when they think they are safe, midnight."
