I do not want to put something random in them as they are very good hard back books, so I will have to think carefully. My first idea was to use them as periodic notes or whatever the small notes with one topic per note is called. And unless I can think of something else, I will go with that. Now the issue bares its head again, what do I write in them? There is more organization I can do to distract myself from this question, the black notebooks are asking for something art related to be in them. So I can do something similar with the rest. Graphing paper can be used for STEM or maybe just math. The lined can be used for Language or other written concepts. And dotted can be used for anything, worldbuilding or science.
